How we work on a live programme

A small firm is a different proposition from a large one, and it is worth being straight about which parts of that help you.

  • The person quoting is the person on siteNothing gets lost between the estimator and the crew, because they are not different people.
  • Licensing is in houseLEW services are not subcontracted out, so sign off does not wait on a third party's schedule.
  • Variations are recorded as they happenConfirmed in writing at the time rather than argued about at final account.
  • We say no when a programme does not fitA firm this size cannot take everything at once, and telling you early is worth more than taking the job.

What to send for a price

Layout drawings or a bill of quantities, the programme dates, the site address, and anything unusual about access or existing conditions. If drawings are not ready, photographs of the existing installation and a description of the scope are usually enough for a budget figure.
