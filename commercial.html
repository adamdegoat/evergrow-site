Why the licence matters to you

In Singapore, electrical installations above a certain size must be covered by an electrical installation licence, and that licence has a Licensed Electrical Worker named against it. The LEW is responsible for the installation being safe and properly tested.

For a building owner or occupier that means two practical things. Work on a licensed installation cannot simply be handed to whoever is cheapest, and the licence carries obligations that continue every year whether or not anything has gone wrong. Both sit inside what we already do.
