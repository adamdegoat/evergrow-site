Who we work for

  1. Renovation contractors and interior designers

    Electrical packages taken as a subcontract, priced off your drawings and worked to your programme. First and second fix, rewiring on occupied buildings, boards and load changes.

    Send drawings or a bill of quantities with the programme dates.

  2. Businesses and building owners

    Licensed work on premises that have to stay compliant. Installations, periodic inspection and testing, fit outs, supply and load upgrades, fault finding and maintenance.

    Tell us the premises type and send a photo of the board.

  3. Homeowners

    HDB, condo and landed. Rewiring, DB box upgrades, lighting and power points, water heater and appliance circuits, and tripping nobody can trace.

    A photo of the DB box with the cover open gets you a realistic range.

LEW services, in house

Certain electrical work in Singapore has to be carried out or supervised by a Licensed Electrical Worker, and many premises must hold an installation licence with an LEW named on it. Ours is in house rather than subcontracted, so a sign off does not wait on somebody else's schedule.
