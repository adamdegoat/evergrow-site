LEW services, in house

Certain electrical work in Singapore has to be carried out or supervised by a Licensed Electrical Worker, and many premises have to hold an electrical installation licence with an LEW named on it. We have that covered without subcontracting it out, which is the difference between a job that can be signed off and one that cannot.

  • Licensed installation work Work that has to be carried out or supervised by a Licensed Electrical Worker.
  • Periodic inspection and testing Inspection and testing of licensed installations on the cycle the licence requires.
  • Applications and submissions The paperwork that goes with licensed work, handled rather than handed back to you.
  • Supply and load changes Where a change in supply or connected load needs a licensed sign off.
