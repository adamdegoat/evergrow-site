# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences arrive on different errands and must each find their answer inside one screen of scrolling.

Main contractors, interior design firms and M&E coordinators are deciding whether to send drawings for a subcontract package. They screen on scope range, licensing and whether the sub will hold a programme. Their context is a site office in the morning, usually on a phone.

Businesses, landlords and facility managers need licensed work and compliance: LEW services, periodic inspection and testing, switchboard work, and someone who answers when a board trips out of hours.

Homeowners want a licensed electrician for rewiring, DB upgrades, power and lighting, or a fault they cannot place. Their context is often urgent and after dark.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for Evergrow Electrical Service, an electrical contractor operating in Singapore since 1986, covering industrial, commercial and residential work from new installation to rewiring, with LEW services in house. It exists to convert enquiries that currently arrive only by word of mouth, and to let the firm be found and screened by buyers who have never met them. Success is a steady flow of qualified WhatsApp and phone enquiries, weighted toward direct commercial and residential work rather than subcontract work alone.

## Positioning

Forty years of licensed electrical contracting across every building type in Singapore, from a firm small enough that the person who quotes the job is on site for it.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: WhatsApp 8321 9747. Secondary: phone call on the same number, with email as the fallback for main contractors sending drawings.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: electrical contracting in Singapore since 1986, industrial to residential, licensed.
- Belief ladder: this firm is real and has been for a long time → they work on buildings like mine → they are properly licensed and can sign off the work → reaching them is one tap.
- Proof on hand: established 1986 (confirmed). Pending from the client: real job photos, a works log of past projects, bizSAFE level, BCA CRS registration. Nothing beyond confirmed facts may appear on the site.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, load bearing, unglamorous. The voice of a tradesman who has done this for four decades and does not need to sell hard: plain statements of capability, no adjectives, no superlatives. Confidence comes from specificity and from the date, not from claims. Should feel like a firm that turns up.

## Anti-references

Not the modal Singapore contractor site: white background, electrical blue and hi-vis yellow, a stock photo of a stranger in a hard hat, three identical service cards, and a form nobody fills in. Not a startup landing page. Not warm cream paper with a ruled editorial document look, which is the current generated-site default. No stock portrait presented as the team. No emoji, no em dashes, no invented credentials.

## Design Principles

State capability as fact, never as a claim. Every assertion on the page is something the firm can prove.

Density is the courtesy. Visitors are deciding, not browsing, so answer the screening question in the first screen rather than delaying it behind a hero.

Colour and material come from the trade. Green from the earth conductor and the firm's own name, copper from what is inside every cable they pull. Nothing decorative that the trade would not recognise.

Longevity is the strongest asset and it is a date, not an adjective. 1986 does the work that testimonials would otherwise have to do.

One tap to a human. Every section ends within reach of WhatsApp, because the buyer is on a phone and often in a hurry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at or above 4.5:1, minimum 16px throughout, full keyboard operability with visible focus, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation. Bilingual English and 中文, since a meaningful share of both the commercial decision makers and the older residential customers read Chinese.
