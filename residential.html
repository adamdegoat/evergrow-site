What we do in homes

Plain descriptions, because most people calling an electrician are not trying to learn the trade.

  • RewiringReplacing old wiring in a flat or house, usually during a renovation or when the existing wiring has reached the end of its life.
  • DB box upgradesThe box by your front door with the switches in it. Replaced or enlarged when it is old, full, or no longer protecting the circuits properly.
  • Lighting and power pointsAdding, moving or replacing sockets, switches, ceiling points and light fittings.
  • Water heaters and appliance circuitsDedicated circuits for water heaters, ovens, hobs and air conditioning, sized for what the appliance actually draws.
  • Tripping and fault findingWhen the power keeps going off and nobody can say why. We trace it rather than guess at it.
  • Renovation electrical worksThe electrical portion of a renovation, working alongside your contractor or interior designer.

Before you call anyone

Two things are worth knowing, whether or not you use us.

  • Electrical work in a home is licensed workIt is worth asking anyone quoting you whether they are licensed, and it is a fair question to ask us too.
  • Repeated tripping is information, not a nuisanceA breaker that keeps going is usually doing its job. Resetting it over and over is not a fix, and the underlying fault does not improve on its own.

What to send us

A photograph of the DB box with the cover open, a photograph of whatever is not working, your property type and your area. Most jobs can be given a realistic range from that, and the ones that cannot are the ones where guessing a price would be doing you no favours.
